───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              PRODUCT LINES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DuPont™ Tedlar® is a portfolio of polyvinyl fluoride (PVF) protective films
with 60+ years of proven performance. Key product lines include:

• Tedlar® Clear Protection (TCP) Film
  Clear protective overlaminate for outdoor signage and graphics.
  Lifespan: 12-20+ years depending on application.

• Tedlar® PVF Films for Graphics
  High-conformability films for vehicle wraps, curved surfaces, and embossed
  products. Used in fleet graphics, racing liveries, and transit exteriors.

• Tedlar® Anti-Graffiti Laminates
  Partnership product with General Formulations (GF 108). Designed for urban
  signage, transit infrastructure, and public spaces.

• Tedlar® Wallcoverings
  Type II wallcovering with Type III durability. Bleach-cleanable, mold/mildew
  resistant. Primary use: healthcare, hospitality, commercial interiors.

• Tedlar® Architectural Films
  Protective films for building facades, metal panels, and exterior cladding.
  Used in coastal and high-corrosion environments.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                           VALUE PROPOSITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

• Extreme Durability: 12-20+ year outdoor lifespan (industry-leading)
• UV Protection: No yellowing, fading, cracking, or color degradation
• Graffiti Resistance: Spray paint, permanent markers, stickers clean off easily
• Chemical Resistance: Withstands bleach, alcohol, harsh industrial cleaners
• Mold & Mildew Resistance: Critical for healthcare and humid environments
• High Conformability: Flexible for vehicle wraps and curved surfaces
• Non-PFAS Composition: Safer material, increasingly important for procurement
• Reduced TCO: Fewer replacements, lower maintenance = long-term cost savings

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                        PRIMARY CUSTOMER SEGMENTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SEGMENT 1: SIGN MANUFACTURERS & FABRICATORS
───────────────────────────────────────────
Profile: Companies that produce outdoor signage, billboards, building murals,
         and large-format graphics for commercial and municipal clients.
Size: $10M-500M+ revenue | 20-500+ employees
Pain Points:
  • Warranty claims from UV fading and weather damage
  • Graffiti vandalism requiring costly replacements
  • Customer complaints about premature sign degradation
  • Need for premium/differentiated product offerings
Decision-Makers: VP/Director of Product, Director of Operations, Owner
Events: ISA Sign Expo, PRINTING United, FESPA
Example: Sign fabrication shops, national sign companies, print-to-install firms

SEGMENT 2: VEHICLE WRAP SPECIALISTS
────────────────────────────────────
Profile: Companies specializing in fleet graphics, commercial vehicle wraps,
         and custom automotive wraps. Often regional players with installer teams.
Size: $5M-100M revenue | 10-200 employees
Pain Points:
  • Film conformability issues on curved surfaces
  • Durability complaints from fleet customers
  • Labor costs for re-wraps and warranty work
  • Competitive differentiation in crowded market
Decision-Makers: Owner/GM, Director of Production, Lead Installer/Trainer
Events: ISA Sign Expo, SEMA, FESPA, Wrap competitions
Example: Fleet graphics companies, franchise wrap shops, motorsports livery

SEGMENT 3: PROTECTIVE FILM CONVERTERS & DISTRIBUTORS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Profile: B2B companies that convert, laminate, and distribute graphic films
         to sign shops and installers. Often white-label or co-brand products.
Size: $50M-500M+ revenue | National or global distribution
Pain Points:
  • Need for differentiated premium product line
  • Demand for longer-lasting overlaminates from customers
  • Competitive pressure from commodity film suppliers
  • Customer requests for anti-graffiti and specialty films
Decision-Makers: VP Product Development, Director of Sourcing, VP Marketing
Events: ISA Sign Expo, FESPA, PRINTING United
Example: General Formulations (current partner), Drytac, Mactac, Arlon

SEGMENT 4: WALLCOVERING & INTERIOR SOLUTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────
Profile: Manufacturers serving healthcare, hospitality, and commercial
         interiors with high-performance wall surfaces.
Size: $20M-300M revenue | Often specialized in institutional markets
Pain Points:
  • Infection control requirements (bleach cleanability)
  • Stain resistance for high-traffic areas
  • Durability to reduce replacement cycles
  • Meeting healthcare facility standards
Decision-Makers: VP Product, Director of R&D, Healthcare Market Manager
Events: Healthcare Design Expo, BDNY, NeoCon, HCD Conference
Example: WallQuest (current partner), healthcare interior specialists

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                       UNIVERSAL QUALIFYING CRITERIA
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Use these criteria to score and qualify companies across all segments:

Revenue Threshold:
  • Tier 1: $50M+ revenue (enterprise, high priority)
  • Tier 2: $10M-50M revenue (mid-market, strong fit)
  • Tier 3: $5M-10M revenue (emerging, selective pursuit)
  • Below $5M: Generally too small unless strategic

Geographic Focus:
  • Primary: US-based companies
  • Secondary: Global companies with US operations
  • Positive signal: Multi-location or national presence

Buying Signals (look for these):
  • Exhibits at ISA Sign Expo, PRINTING United, FESPA, or Healthcare Design
  • Active in ISA, SGIA, or USGBC associations
  • Markets "outdoor," "durable," or "long-lasting" products
  • Emphasizes quality, premium positioning, or extended warranties
  • Recent product launches in outdoor/architectural/healthcare space
  • Mentions competitor laminates (3M, Avery, Oracal) on website

Disqualification Signals:
  • Pure retail/B2C (no manufacturing or converting)
  • Only indoor/short-term applications
  • No evidence of film/laminate usage
  • Too small with no growth trajectory

                            TARGET PERSONAS

Priority 1: VP/Director of Product Development
  → Owns product roadmap, influences material selection

Priority 2: VP/Director of R&D or Innovation
  → Evaluates new materials, runs trials and testing

Priority 3: VP/Director of Operations or Manufacturing
  → Cares about process efficiency, material performance

Priority 4: Director of Procurement or Sourcing
  → Manages supplier relationships, negotiates contracts

Priority 5: Owner/GM (at smaller companies)
  → Makes all major decisions, often technical background

Priority 6: VP/Director of Marketing (if product-focused)
  → Influences product positioning and premium offerings
